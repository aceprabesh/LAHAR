Born From Movement

LAHAR was born in Kathmandu, Nepal — a city where ancient tradition meets modern ambition. We take our name from the Nepali word for wave, a reminder that style, like water, finds its own path.

Our founder, returning home after years abroad, saw a gap in the market for contemporary fashion that honors Nepali craftsmanship while speaking to the global woman. LAHAR is that bridge — heritage meets contemporary.

We work with local artisans who understand fabric as intimately as we understand women. Each piece is designed to move with you, to flow with your day, to become part of your story.

What We Stand For

Artisan Crafted

Every LAHAR piece is crafted by skilled Nepali artisans. We believe in fair wages, safe working conditions, and preserving traditional textile craftsmanship.

Sustainable Materials

We prioritize natural fibers — linen, cotton, silk — sourced responsibly. Our packaging is plastic-free, and we're committed to reducing our environmental footprint.

Inclusive Design

Fashion should celebrate every body. Our sizes range from XS to XL, and we're expanding. Our designs flatter diverse figures and embrace all identities.

A Word From Our Founder

"I started LAHAR because I wanted to wear my heritage, not hide it. I wanted clothes that moved like I did — from Kathmandu mornings to global evenings."

After studying fashion design in London and working in Paris, I returned home to Nepal. I saw the incredible craftsmanship here — generations of textile knowledge — and I wanted to share it with the world.

LAHAR is my love letter to Nepal, to women everywhere, and to the idea that we can all wear our own wave.
