The LAHAR Story

"Born from movement, shaped by individuality."

LAHAR draws inspiration from the Nepali word for wave — a reminder that style, like water, finds its own path. We create clothing that moves with you, not against you.

Each piece is designed in Kathmandu, crafted for the modern woman who values both heritage and contemporary expression.
